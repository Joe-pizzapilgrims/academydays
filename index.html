What are Academy Days?

Academy Days take place over the course of a whole day and are designed around specific groups of Pilgrims, from Waiters and Pizza Chefs through to our future managers and leaders.

Throughout the day you'll explore a mixture of practical skills, behaviours and leadership techniques, supported by different members of Pizza Base and experienced people from across the business.

They're your opportunity to practise, ask questions, learn from others and show us what you're capable of. Most importantly, they're a space to really demonstrate our Push Yourself and Enjoy Yourself values.

How do I get invited?

Academy Days are invite only and form part of your development pathway at Pizza Pilgrims.

Once you've reached 90% on your current role Deep Dive and achieved the required level on your Development Tracker, you'll become eligible for the next Academy Day for your cohort. An invitation will then be triggered for an upcoming workshop.

Before you can be fully signed off into your next role, you'll need to attend the Academy workshop designed for your level. Think of it as the bridge between proving what you can do in your current role and preparing yourself for what comes next.

Your Academy Pathway

Academy Days develop alongside you as your career progresses, with different workshops aimed at different stages of your Pizza Pilgrims journey.

1. Waiters & Pizza Chefs
2. Seniors & Supervisors
3. Assistant Managers & Second Chefs

For General Managers and Head Chefs, development doesn't stop once you've reached the top of the pathway.

Area Meetings, Super 6'ers, Head Chef meetings and other development sessions are all opportunities to keep building your skills, sharing ideas and learning from the wider business. These sessions will now be treated as part of your Academy journey too.
